Start with the "six weeks later" test

Before you click buy, ask one question: will she still be using this six weeks after the baby arrives? Most baby shower gifts fail this test. Newborn-size clothing is gone in a fortnight. Decorative items end up in the cupboard. The gifts that survive — and become the ones she remembers — are the ones that keep showing up in everyday life and in photos for the first year and beyond.

For a baby girl specifically, hair accessories sit at the top of that list. They start being used the moment she's home from hospital (soft headbands for milestone photos), grow with her into the bow and clip stage, and photograph beautifully across every season. They're also under R600 in their nicest form, which is exactly where SA shower gifting lives.

Don't forget the mother

Most SA baby shower guides treat baby shower gifts for the mother as an afterthought — a separate add-on or an awkward "and a little something for you." It doesn't have to be that way. A Mom & Me piece is a gift for both. So is a hand-written card that says something specific about who she is and why she'll be a wonderful mom (more on that in our thoughtful gifts piece). And practically — a UCook voucher or a pre-paid takeaway slot for week three is, by some distance, the most underrated baby shower gift for the mother in SA right now.

What to skip

  • 0–3 month clothing. She'll have ten of everything in this size already, and half will be outgrown before opening.
  • Generic white onesies. She has them.
  • Battery-operated noisy toys. No new mom needs more sound in the house at 3am.
  • Wire-frame headbands or stiff plastic clips for newborns. Soft fabric or nylon only at this stage — see are baby hair clips safe for the full safety breakdown.
  • Anything that looks generic in the gift photo. The mom will post a thank-you slide. Make sure your gift looks like itself in that photo.

How to write the card

The card often outlasts the gift. Skip "congratulations" — she'll get fifty of those. Write what you wish for this baby girl specifically. Tell her mom-to-be one true thing about who she is and why she's already a great mother. Add a future date: "Coffee on a Saturday in July, my treat — text me when you're ready." The actual booking is a gift in itself.

"The best baby shower gift for a girl says: I see your daughter as her own person, and I see you as a mother. Both, on the same gift table."
